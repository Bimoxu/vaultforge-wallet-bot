# VaultForgeWalletBot

Your secure self-custodial crypto wallet inside Telegram.

## What is VaultForgeWalletBot?

VaultForgeWalletBot is a multi-chain crypto wallet that runs directly in Telegram. It lets you manage your digital assets, track balances, and send or receive cryptocurrencies easily—all without leaving the app.

## Features

- **Self-Custodial**: You own your private keys. No third party controls your funds.
- **Multi-Chain Support**: Manage Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and more from a single bot.
- **Advanced Security**: Strong encryption and regular updates keep your assets safe.
- **Effortless Transactions**: Check balances, send, and receive crypto with simple commands.

## How to Use

1. Open Telegram and search for `@VaultForgeWalletBot707_bot`.
2. Start the bot and follow the instructions.
3. Create your wallet and start managing your crypto.
